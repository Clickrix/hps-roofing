How it works

Our installation process

The same careful steps on every roof — no shortcuts, no surprises.

Free inspection

We assess your roof and review your insurance claim (or cash options) so you know exactly where you stand.

Protect your property

Tarps and plywood shield landscaping, siding, and everything around your home before work begins.

Strip to the decking

Old roofing comes off completely so damaged decking can be found and replaced — never roofed over.

Seal it right

Synthetic underlayment, starter strip for wind resistance, drip edge, and replaced valley metal.

Install & ventilate

Architectural shingles, matched venting, and ridge venting where space allows for a longer roof life.

Spotless cleanup

Debris blown off, gutters cleared, and the whole property swept with a magnet for nails.
